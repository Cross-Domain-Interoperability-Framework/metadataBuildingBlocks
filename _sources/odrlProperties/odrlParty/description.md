## ODRL Party

W3C ODRL 2.2 Party (a person, organization or agent identified by IRI) or PartyCollection (a group of parties, identified by @id, or by odrl:source narrowed with odrl:refinement). Used for the assigner and assignee of ODRL rules and policies.

Generic W3C ODRL; CDIF-specific rules belong in profile blocks such as [odrlOffer](../odrlOffer/).

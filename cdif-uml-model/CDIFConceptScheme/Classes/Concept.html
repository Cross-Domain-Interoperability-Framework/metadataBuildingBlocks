CDIFConceptScheme::Classes::Concept

Definition

Documentation ============= Original -------- Reference ......... skos:Concept Definition .......... An idea or notion; a unit of thought. CDIF ---- Definition .......... A SKOS Concept member of a ConceptScheme. Must have @type (containing skos:Concept), skos:prefLabel, and skos:notation. Optionally carries skos:definition, skos:note, skos:inScheme (back-pointer to its scheme), and hierarchical skos:broader / skos:narrower links to other concepts.
